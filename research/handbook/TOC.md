Revised May 2025."* PDF metadata: created 2026-04-22, modified 2026-09-10. 108 physical PDF pages (cover + front matter printed pp.1–7 + 7 chapters + Notes/back matter printed pp.103–106).
- **Page markers:** `[p.N]` = the PDF's own **printed** page number (running header/footer). Physical PDF page = printed page + 1 for body pages (cover is unnumbered).
