# Exam-day logistics — NC learner permit knowledge/signs test (official sources only)

All facts below were read from ncdot.gov pages on 2026-09-27. NOT FOUND items are marked.

1. **Bring (standard list, learner permit):** one document proving age & identity (full name + DOB); a Social Security card **or** one document proving your SSN; U.S. citizens: one document proving NC residency (a REAL ID additionally requires **two** proofs of NC residency); non-U.S. citizens: one U.S.-government document proving legal presence. — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/new-drivers/Pages/license-learner-permit.aspx and https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/nc-real-id/Pages/requirements.aspx
2. **Full document lists:** DL-231 "Documents You May Need at the Driver License Office" (Rev 6-2025) — Table 1 identity (2 forms required), Table 2 SSN, Table 3 NC residency, Table 5 legal presence. — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/downloads/Documents/DL-231.pdf
3. **Non-U.S. citizens / international students:** F-1/F-2/M-1/M-2 → I-20 with I-94 (or unexpired foreign passport w/ CBP admission stamp); J-1/J-2 → DS-2019 with I-94 (or passport stamp); also accepted: electronic I-94, I-551 green card, I-766 EAD, I-797 receipt notice with supporting doc. — DL-231 Table 5 above; https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/help/Pages/proving-legal-presence.aspx
4. **SSN rule:** SSN required; DMV verifies it with the Social Security Administration. If you are **not eligible** for an SSN, provide a document indicating legal presence instead. An "SSN denial letter" is **NOT FOUND** in the official accepted lists (accepted SSN proofs: SS card, 1099, W-2, payroll record, SSA document, Medicare card, DD-214, military record). — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/help/Pages/proving-social-security.aspx
5. **Insurance:** proof of liability insurance is **not required for a learner's permit** (it is required for a driver license; no electronic copy). — learner-permit page (see 1)
6. **Appointment:** driver license offices take appointments up to 7 days ahead (new slots released each weekday); after booking you must confirm by text/email **within 15 minutes** or it is released; arrive 5–10 min early, arriving ≥15 min late auto-cancels. Book at https://skiptheline.ncdot.gov/ (linked from https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-license-appointments/Pages/default.aspx)
7. **Fee:** Level 1 limited learner permit **$25.50** (official fee table). Pay by common payment methods; online PayIt transactions add $3 + 1.85% card fee. — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/new-drivers/Pages/level1.aspx and https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/Pages/licenses-fees.aspx (adult Class C learner permit fee row lives in the JS-rendered fee table — check in a browser)
8. **Tests given at the office:** written knowledge (traffic laws & safe driving; offered in different languages, oral on request), **traffic signs** (identify by color/shape and explain each), and **vision**; the driving-skills test comes later (Level 2 / full license). — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/new-drivers/Pages/driver-license-tests.aspx
9. **Retake rule:** applicants who fail the Class C knowledge or driving test may retake it in **7 calendar days**. — same page as 8
10. **Teens 15–17 (Level 1):** also bring Driver Eligibility Certificate + Driver Education Certificate and have a parent/guardian present; adults 18+ apply for a learner permit directly. Permit is issued centrally and mailed (Temporary Driving Certificate in the meantime); applying cancels other states' permits/licenses via State-to-State Verification (bring the old credential if any). — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/new-drivers/Pages/level1.aspx and learner-permit page (see 1)

NOT FOUND: explicit "SSN denial letter" acceptance (see 4); explicit adult Class C learner-permit fee row (fee table is JS-rendered; Level 1 $25.50 verified instead, item 7).
