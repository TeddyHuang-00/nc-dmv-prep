## 1. Test structure

- **(official)** The knowledge (written) test "Covers traffic laws and safe-driving practice" and is a separate test from the traffic-signs test. — Driver License Tests page. Handbook p.8: "Each driver license test must be passed separately."
- **(official)** Traffic-signs test: "Covers regulatory and warning signs; applicant identifies signs by color and shape and provides an explanation of each." — Driver License Tests page.
- **(official)** A learner-permit applicant must pass vision, traffic-signs and knowledge (written) tests; for under-18 applicants "a parent or guardian present for these tests" (or written consent). — level1.aspx; handbook p.8 ("For an original driver license of any type, you may be tested on: Vision; Knowledge of motor vehicle laws; Traffic signs; and Driving skill (on-road test).").
- **(official)** Tests are administered in person at NCDMV driver license offices; skills/road test may also be taken at certified driver-education schools (road test only). — level2.aspx.

## 2. Number of questions / passing score

- **NOT FOUND (official).** Neither the Driver License Tests page, the Level 1 page, nor the handbook states how many questions the knowledge test contains or the passing score.
- **(secondary: https://driving-tests.org/north-carolina/north-carolina-permit-practice-test)** "The written knowledge test has 25 multiple-choice questions on traffic laws and safe driving – you need at least 20 correct (80%) to pass."; "If you fail, you must wait 7 calendar days before retaking."
- **(secondary: https://www.epermittest.com/north-carolina/nc-dmv-practice-test)** "Questions: 25; Correct answers to pass: 20; Passing score: 80%; Test languages: English, Spanish, Hindi."
- **(secondary: https://zutobi.com/us/nc-car/practice-permit-test)** "The North Carolina permit test consists of 25 questions, and you must answer at least 20 questions correctly to get a passing score (80%)."
- **(secondary: https://enlacelatinonc.org/en/north-carolina-drivers-license-test)** "Number of questions: 25 multiple-choice questions. Minimum passing score: 80% (at least 20 correct answers)."
- Consistent across 5+ third-party sites: **25 questions, 20 correct (80%), no time limit; practice app should be built to 25-question / 80% default.** Treat as unconfirmed-official.

## 3. Retakes

- **(official)** "Applicants who do not pass the knowledge test or driving test for a regular Class C license may retake the test in seven calendar days." — Driver License Tests page.

## 4. Fees

- **(official)** Level 1 Limited Learner Permit: **$25.50** — level1.aspx and handbook Schedule of Fees (p.29): "Level 1 - Driving with a limited learner permit … $25.50". (Handbook also lists "Learner Permit … $25.50" under G.S. 20-7.)
- **(official)** Level 2 Limited Provisional License: $25.50 — level2.aspx and handbook p.29.
- **NOT FOUND (official)** a separate "knowledge test fee."
- **(secondary: https://driving-tests.org/north-carolina/north-carolina-permit-practice-test)** "Fee $20 (knowledge test)".

## 6. Appointment

- **(official)** Handbook p.8: "Appointments for driver license examinations can be made online at skiptheline.ncdot.gov, but an appointment is not necessary. Walk-ins also are accepted all day, or until capacity is reached."
- **(official)** Driver License Tests page has a "Make an Appointment" section. Office hours: most offices 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., Mon–Fri (handbook p.8).

## 7. Learner permit eligibility (who sits the knowledge test)

- **(official)** Adult route: "Learner permits are available to persons age 18 and older." — handbook p.8. Licenses & Fees page: "Learner permits are issued for new drivers 18 years old or older and graduated permits and licenses to individuals 15 to 17 years old." — https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/driver-licenses/Pages/licenses-fees.aspx
- **(official)** Teen route (Level 1 Limited Learner Permit): "at least 15 years old but less than 18 years old" (DL-214); handbook p.12: "If you are at least 15 years of age and have completed an approved driver education course … you may apply for a Level One Limited Learner Permit to operate vehicles requiring a Class C License."
- **(official)** Driver-ed requirement: approved course of "at least 30 hours classroom instruction and six hours behind-the wheel instruction", plus a Driver Eligibility Certificate / diploma (handbook p.12; level1.aspx). Enrollment possible from age 14½ (level1.aspx).
- **(official)** Level 2 upgrade: at least 16, held Level 1 for 9 months, no moving/seat-belt/cell-phone convictions in preceding 6 months (DL-214; handbook p.12).

## 8. Level 1 permit — driving restrictions & driving log (testable facts)

- **(official)** Supervised at all times: "A supervising driver must be seated beside the permit holder in the front seat of the vehicle when it is in motion. No person other than the supervising driver is allowed in the front seat." — DL-214.
- **(official)** Hours (first six months): "the permit holder may drive only between the hours of 5 a.m. to 9 p.m., with a supervising driver. After the first six months, the permit holder may drive at any time with a supervising driver." — DL-214; same in handbook p.12 and level1.aspx ("you can drive 5 a.m. to 9 p.m. while supervised for the first six months").
- **(official)** Supervising driver: parent/grandparent/guardian, or an approved responsible person; must be a licensed driver "who has been licensed to drive for at least five years" (DL-214, DL-4A, handbook p.12).
- **(official)** Driving log: "You will be given a Driving Log to be completed detailing a minimum of 60 hours of operation. At least 10 hours must occur during nighttime hours." — handbook p.12; log must be signed by the supervising driver and submitted with the Level 2 application.
- **(official)** DL-4A log form adds: "No more than 10 hours per week may count toward the 60 hours." and "At least 10 of the 60 hours must be at night."
- **(official)** Cell phone: "unlawful for the driver … under the age of 18 to use a mobile/cellular telephone for any purpose" (emergency/LE/parent exceptions) — DL-214, G.S. 20-137.3.
- **(official)** Passengers must be restrained by seat belt/child safety seat (handbook p.12). Level 1 must be held 9 months before applying for Level 2 (handbook p.12, DL-214).
- **(official)** Permit renewal: "can be renewed up to one year after expiration by visiting a driver license office" (no tests retaken) — level1.aspx. Replacement permit card arrives by mail (level1.aspx: "arrive in the mail within 20 business days").
